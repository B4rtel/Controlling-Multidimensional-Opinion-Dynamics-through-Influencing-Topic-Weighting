This repository contains the files used to produce the figures shown in H. Bartel, M. Burger, S. Glendinning, S. N. Gomes, and
M.-T. Wolfram The Importance of Importance: Controlling Multidimensional Opinion Dynamics through Influencing Topic Weighting, 2026.

We used controlling-clustering-runningcost.ipynb to produce the figures in Section 3, controlling-clustering-costatend.ipynb to produce the figures in Section 4 and controlling-clustering-runningcost-multiplemedia.ipynb to produce the figures in Section 4. Additionally, controlling-clustering-verify-adjoint-gradient.ipynb contains figures and checks to verify our adjoint gradient against the finite difference gradient.
